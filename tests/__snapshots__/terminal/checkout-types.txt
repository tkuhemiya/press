
  What checkout and billing publish
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  A class here is how another module talks to this one, not a list of every type
  in the package. Order is checkout’s surface; Payment is billing’s. Neighbours
  call submit() and capture(). They do not import helpers.

  Who may import whom is the import grid (module-deps.md). Groups here use the
  same module names as that grid.

  ① submit() is what a neighbour of checkout calls.
  ② capture() is what a neighbour of billing calls.
  ③ Line items live inside an order; they are not a second surface.

  submit[a] is the command. The diamond on contains is ownership. The hollow
  triangle on implements is the payment protocol billing publishes.
